The development

SNN Estates Felicity is 2, 3 and 4 BHK apartments in nine towers on 6.2 acres behind Manyata Tech Park in Jakkur, with easy access to Hebbal and the airport and close to the tech parks, starting at 1.05 crore. The campaign line was Happiness Begins at Home.

Three content streams

01 Interior photography

I photographed the show flats and designed the property showcase creatives across all unit types, from a 700 sq ft 1.5 BHK to a 2,300 sq ft 4 BHK, each carrying the unit size, the configuration and the campaign line.

02 Property advertising

Multi-format ads communicating project scale, location and pricing across digital platforms: the towers, the starting price, and the living, bedroom and dining interiors.

03 Lifestyle creatives

An amenity storytelling series, copywritten and designed: the pet park, the kids' play area, the Miyawaki forest and the pool.

Film

The campaign also ran on video. I shot and edited a vertical house tour for social in March 2024, published on the Address Masters channel.
