Saginaw Tattoo studio website

A dark, editorial website for a tattoo studio: gallery, artists, style categories and booking, built around large black-and-white photography and a blackletter wordmark.

Role

The idea

Tattoo studio websites usually bury the work under menus. This concept does the opposite: photography fills the page, a blackletter wordmark sits against a modern grotesk for navigation, and every page ends in a way to book.

Pages

About and the artists

The studio's storefront and its story, then the masters: each artist with a photo, years of experience and the styles they work in.

Gallery and contact

A gallery with a large feature image and a strip of thumbnails, then a contact form and a newsletter footer with the booking link repeated.
