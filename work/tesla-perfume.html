A perfume bottle for Tesla

A brand extension exercise: redesign a FOGG perfume bottle as if Tesla made it. A faceted glass bottle in blue with a polished glass cap and a gold metal snuffer.

Program
B.Des Product Design, MIT World Peace University, semester 4
Role
Individual project
Deliverables
Brand research, moodboard, ideation sketches, final concept renders
Links
Full board on Behance

Research

The chosen product was a FOGG perfume and the chosen brand was Tesla. The moodboard of Tesla's interiors and cars showed a sleek, simple language with thoughtful details inside and out, from a brand whose customers are loyal enough to wait years for the next model. Tesla had also already made a bottle: Tesla Tequila, a lightning-bolt glass bottle that sells for around 250 dollars, which set the precedent for an angular form.

Ideation

Final concept

  • Glass cap with a polished finish.
  • Metal snuffer cap in gold.
  • Tesla logo on the front facet.
  • The perfume visible through the blue faceted body.
