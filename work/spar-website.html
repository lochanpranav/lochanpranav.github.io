Spar Agrotech website

A website and identity for a women-led farmer producer company in Pune: the product range, the sustainability story and the board of directors, in green and cream.

The brief

Spar Agrotech is a farmer producer company whose product, NEAV, comes from a vision of opportunity for women in farming. The site had to do three things: sell a range of fruit and vegetable products, explain the company's sustainability practices, and put its women board of directors front and center.

Identity

A serif wordmark with a leaf, a deep green and a cream ground that lets the fruit photography carry the color. The same green and cream run through the site so the brand and the pages read as one thing.

The website

The home page opens with "Spreading the goodness of fruits" and a search, then about, the sustainability principles, and the product range with mango pulp, tomato puree, french fries and papaya pulp. A dedicated page introduces the women board of directors with portraits and the company's commitment to an inclusive workplace. Contact details and the office address close it out.
